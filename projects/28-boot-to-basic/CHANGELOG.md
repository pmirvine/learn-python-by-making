## [0.1.0] - 2026-09-27

### Added

- A computer that boots to BASIC, with a forty-column text screen over the graphics.
- `MODE`, `CLS`, `CLG`, `COLOUR`, `GCOL`, `MOVE`, `DRAW`, `PLOT`, `SOUND` and `ENVELOPE`.
- Sprites: `SPRITE`, `PUT`, `HIDE`, `COLLIDE` and `EDGE`.
- `INKEY`, `TAB` and `WAIT`.
- A disc: `SAVE`, `LOAD` and `CAT`.
- Two programs on the disc: `carpet` and `alien`.
